A concept page explains how Cyclix works and why it works that way. Concepts are for people who run Cyclix and want to know what it does with their issues, board and repository. The steps for a task are in [Guides](/guides/), and the full lists of keys and flags are in [Reference](/reference/).

- [How Cyclix works](/concepts/how-cyclix-works/): what Cyclix does, the stages an issue goes through, the work states, and where Cyclix keeps its data.
